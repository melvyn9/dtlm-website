Nestled in the historic heart of Chinatown, Kuala Lumpur, +N by Ur-Mu is an exemplary project that breathes new life into an old, non-descript 1940s modernist shophouse. Originally a budget backpackers’ hostel before serving as workers’ quarters for a nearby construction site, the building had fallen into a state of severe disrepair. Today, through thoughtful alteration and refurbishment, it has been transformed into a vibrant, multi-functional space that harmonizes history, art, and commerce.

The restoration process focused on preserving the integrity of the original building while introducing modern enhancements. A new lift was installed for accessibility, and damaged mosaic tile flooring was meticulously replaced to maintain its historical charm. New mechanical, electrical, and plumbing services were integrated, along with upgraded toilets, ensuring the building meets contemporary standards while respecting its past.

At street level, a charming florist and café now occupy the ground and first floors, inviting both locals and visitors into a serene urban retreat. This commercial space adds a fresh and welcoming presence to the neighborhood, complementing the artistic vision that defines the upper levels of the building.

The second to fourth floors house Ur-Mu (Urban Museum), a contemporary art space showcasing the esteemed collection of renowned art collector Ng Sek San. The museum serves as a cultural hub, providing an intimate setting for modern art lovers to engage with thought-provoking works in an urban context. By repurposing a once-forgotten building, Ur-Mu redefines how contemporary art can exist within the fabric of Kuala Lumpur’s historical landscape.

Crowning the transformation is a rooftop water garden, a tranquil escape from the bustling city below. This green sanctuary, featuring water plants and fish life, offers a unique connection to nature while enhancing the sustainability of the building.

A striking new high-street façade completes the project, featuring white perforated steel and white glass screens. This modern intervention not only revitalizes the building’s exterior but also creates a dynamic contrast between the old and the new, reinforcing the project’s narrative of adaptive reuse.

+N by Ur-Mu is more than just a refurbishment; it is a statement on sustainability, heritage conservation, and contemporary urban living. By blending history with innovation, it stands as a model for how forgotten spaces can be reimagined into vibrant cultural landmarks that enrich the city
