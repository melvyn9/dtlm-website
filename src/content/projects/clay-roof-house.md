A residence on a modest lot in the older suburbs of Petaling Jaya, built by
demolishing and rebuilding an existing structure. The original clay roof tiles
were salvaged and repurposed as a facade element: vertical steel rods carry the
tiles and allow them to pivot freely, reducing solar heat gain through the
windows and glass doors behind.

At night, with the internal lights on, the tiles read from the street as a soft,
tremulous screen.

The architecture works through a purism in the raw walls and the use of building
materials in their natural form, combined with minimalist principles and
functional green systems.
